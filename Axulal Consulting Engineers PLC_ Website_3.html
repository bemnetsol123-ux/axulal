About Axulal

Born in 2011 inside the national rural roads program, Axulal is now a Category-1 consultant for highways and bridges.

Two engineers and one surveyor, each with more than fifteen years in the industry, founded the firm. We cover the full run of a job: study, survey, design, tender documents, supervision and contract administration.

We use locally available resources and techniques, and we involve the people who will use the road, women and men, young and old, in rural and urban areas alike.

Vision

To push past the default standard of consulting in Ethiopia, and set a new brand for the country through well-studied solutions.

Mission

To deliver quality services that exceed client expectations, through continuous training and innovation.

Led by experience

Our General Manager has more than 17 years in road and infrastructure engineering in Ethiopia.

A team for each job

Every project gets a dedicated team under a senior specialist, with draftsmen and surveyors added as needed.

Three departments

Design, Contract Administration, and Administration and Finance, under one General Manager.

What we do

Road and bridge engineering is our specialty, backed by the studies, surveys and site supervision that go with it.
